\paragraph{The post-training signal.}
Localization does not only name where the failure is. It names a different
post-training target for each failure, and a different patient for each target.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt

\item \textbf{Below 4B the belief is already sufficient, and the mapping is the
entire deficit.} \model{Gemma-2} 2B, \model{Llama-3.2} 3B and
\model{Phi-4-mini} hold a linearly decodable belief in the reactive games ---
\(84.9\) to \(88.6\) on \textit{Matching Pennies} --- while their action
agreement there is exactly \(50.0\): constant sign responders whose decision
does not vary at all with evidence they demonstrably represent, and which point
in opposite directions on identical histories. Steering gives them no lift over
a matched random direction, so there is no gain to turn up. The belief is
instead available directly as a supervised target for a utility sensitive
mapping that has to be \emph{built}. This is the paper's strongest case against
scale as the remedy: these models do not need to be taught the structure of the
game, they need the link, and adding parameters addresses a stage that is not
failing.

\item \textbf{At 27B and above the mapping exists but is not reliable, and
chain of thought is a fragile substitute for it.} The largest models reach
\(83.9\) to \(93.4\) on the game theoretic scenarios, but the correct action
frequently survives only as a marginal preference that the model's own
reasoning then recovers --- and reasoning is precisely what fails on the
reactive games. \model{Llama-3.3} 70B \emph{loses} accuracy under chain of
thought on \textit{Stag Hunt}, \(73.5\) to \(71.0\), the only large model cell
where reasoning hurts, and \model{Qwen-3.5} 4B's apparent recovery there is
selection over the \(59.2\%\) of steps it terminates at all. A mapping repaired
only at inference time, by a serialized computation that itself drifts in the
scenarios that require conditioning on the opponent's response, is not a
repaired mapping. Post-training on the belief to action link is what makes the
correct action the model's first commitment rather than something it argues its
way back to.

\item \textbf{Belief formation is itself trainable under game theoretic
structure, and the layer curves say where to act.} Belief is not at ceiling in
these scenarios: the reactive family reaches \(90.2\) at the final layer
against \(99.4\) for Bayesian inference and \(98.5\) for the nonreactive game,
and --- unlike every nonreactive scenario, which saturates by roughly half
depth and stays flat --- it is still climbing at the last layer and never
plateaus. A representation that never consolidates is a second, independent
target, and the depth profile is itself the supervision signal: it identifies
which layers a belief shaping objective should act on, rather than tuning the
network uniformly and hoping the belief sharpens as a side effect.

\end{itemize}

These are three distinct checkpoints --- mapping construction below 4B, mapping
reliability above 27B, belief consolidation under opponent reactivity --- and
the same diagnostic assigns each model to the one it needs. That is the
practical content of localization. It also makes game theoretic post-training
of \emph{small} models a well posed direction rather than an optimistic one:
the supervision target is present in the representation, measurable before
training, and measurable again after, with belief accuracy, action agreement
and rescue rate as separate endpoints that can distinguish a mapping that was
built from a belief that was merely strengthened. What it argues against is the
uniform response --- more parameters, more instruction tuning, or a reasoning
model --- none of which touch the stage the measurements identify as broken.
