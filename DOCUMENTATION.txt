================================================================================
0.  ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY
================================================================================

This pipeline asks WHERE decision failure enters an LLM that faces sequentially
accumulating evidence in Bayesian and game-theoretic environments. The decision
pipeline of the model is decomposed as

    history --> latent belief --> action preference --> CoT explanation
            --> final answer

and each stage gets its own instrument: (i) a PRE-CoT forced-choice logit read
at the exact position where the model would emit its answer letter, before a
single reasoning token is generated; (ii) a POST-CoT answer-token logit read at
the generated 'Answer: A/B' position after the model has written its chain of
thought; (iii) the full residual stream at the last prompt token, saved for
offline linear probing of the latent belief; and (iv) the parsed behavioral
answer. Every environment ships an EXACT (or documented near-exact) Bayesian
oracle, so at each round t we know the normative P(optimal action | h_t). The
central quantity is the divergence between the normative trajectory and the
model's pre-CoT / post-CoT preference trajectories over t — "decisional drift"
— plus the amplification Delta_amp = Delta_post - Delta_pre, which localizes
whether the failure is (a) the history never entering the representation,
(b) a correct belief mapped to the wrong action, (c) CoT amplifying a wrong or
absent prior, (d) CoT corrupting a correct prior, or (e) a mere output-format
artifact that must never be confused with a scientific finding.

================================================================================
1.  SCIENTIFIC MOTIVATION AND THE DRIFT HYPOTHESIS
================================================================================

1.1  The normative picture
--------------------------
In every environment here, a hidden session parameter theta (host rule, arm
quality, opponent type, trustee disposition) is fixed before round 1 and never
changes. Evidence about theta arrives one round at a time. A Bayesian agent
maintains a posterior P(theta | h_t) and chooses the action maximizing expected
utility under that posterior. Two properties follow:

  (N1) INFORMATIVENESS: as t grows, h_t pins down theta, so the posterior
       concentrates and the optimal-action probability moves away from 0.5
       (except in adversarial trajectories, e.g. TFT opponents reacting to an
       alternating partner — see 6.3 — where the normative curve legitimately
       oscillates; that is a feature, not a bug: it tests TRACKING, not just
       convergence).

  (N2) MONOTONE EVIDENCE RESPONSE: the direction of preference change is
       dictated by the evidence, not by when the evidence arrives.

1.2  The drift hypothesis
-------------------------
DECISIONAL DRIFT := divergence between the normative evidence-conditioned
action trajectory and the model's latent or expressed action trajectory.

Concretely, with oracle_p(t) = P(act1 optimal | h_t) and the model's pre-CoT
semantic gap Delta_pre(t) (section 11), drift manifests as any of:

  D1  FLAT      : oracle_p moves substantially, Delta_pre stays flat
                  -> accumulated history never enters the model's
                     action-relevant representation ("prior fixation").
  D2  REVERSED  : Delta_pre moves against the evidence
                  -> early rounds installed a wrong prior that later evidence
                     strengthens rather than corrects.
  D3  SATURATED : Delta_pre pinned at an extreme from t=1 regardless of
                  history content -> the model answers from a task-level
                  prior (e.g. "always switch in Monty Hall") rather than from
                  the session evidence.
  D4  DECAYING  : |Delta_pre| shrinks with t although oracle_p sharpens
                  -> genuine context-overload signature (the original v1
                     hypothesis; so far NOT observed — see section 2).

1.3  Why probing multiple stages matters
----------------------------------------
A wrong final answer is compatible with at least four different root causes.
Scoring only final answers (all prior versions of this project) cannot
distinguish them. The measurement stack in section 4 is designed so that each
root cause produces a distinct signature across the instruments:

  stage failed                pre-CoT gap      post-CoT gap     behavior
  -------------------------   --------------   --------------   --------
  belief update (D1/D2/D3)    wrong/flat       wrong            wrong
  action mapping              CORRECT          wrong            wrong
  CoT corruption              CORRECT          wrong (flipped)  wrong
  CoT rationalization         weak/wrong       LARGE wrong      wrong
  format artifact             (any)            INVALID          unmeasurable

This converts "LLMs are bad at sequential reasoning" (weak, over-claimed) into
"the failure enters at stage X, with signature Y, in model Z" (localized,
falsifiable, reviewable).

1.4  Connection to prior work (for the paper's related-work section)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pre-CoT probing: recent work shows final answers are often decodable from
  residual-stream activations BEFORE any chain-of-thought is generated,
  supporting the concern that CoT can rationalize an already-formed decision.
- CoT faithfulness: natural-language explanations can misrepresent the causal
  basis of the answer. Our Delta_amp metric quantifies one specific,
  measurable aspect of this: how much the generated reasoning moves the
  action logit, and in which direction relative to the evidence.
- Belief-state geometry: transformers can linearly represent belief states
  over hidden generative processes; our environments are exactly hidden
  generative processes, so the linear probe of section 13 is the natural
  instrument.
- Tuned lens / logit lens: layer-wise decoding of hidden states; we implement
  the raw logit-lens variant with explicit caveats (10.5) and treat the
  trained linear probe as the paper-grade instrument.

We do NOT claim to have invented pre-CoT probing. The contribution is the
DIAGNOSTIC COMBINATION applied to temporal Bayesian / game-theoretic decision
making with exact oracles, plus the failure taxonomy.

================================================================================
2.  WHY THE PREVIOUS PROBE WAS INSUFFICIENT
================================================================================

The Monty pipeline (Drift /Monty) and bandit v1 read the logit gap at the
generated answer token, i.e. AFTER the model wrote its reasoning. Three
independent problems were identified empirically across the v1-v3 runs:

2.1  Post-rationalization saturation
------------------------------------
By the time the model writes 'Answer:', its own CoT tokens have already loaded
the residual stream with evidence for one option. The measured gap therefore
reflects COMMITMENT TO THE GENERATED ARGUMENT, not calibrated uncertainty
about the inference problem. Observed signature: |gap| ~ 8-15 nats (softmax
~100%) on nearly every valid round, regardless of the actual ambiguity of the
evidence. The temporal signal the benchmark was designed to elicit is
structurally invisible at this measurement point.

2.2  The scores[-1] fallback is garbage
---------------------------------------
When the model never wrote 'Answer: A/B' (chronic for Llama-3.2-3B in the
shifting condition), the old pipeline fell back to the logits at the LAST
GENERATED TOKEN — an arbitrary prose position. The A/B logit gap there
measures nothing about the decision. All [NO A/B] measurements from v1-v3 are
unreliable and are excluded from any analysis. In this pipeline the fallback
is REMOVED: an invalid round carries no post-CoT gap, full stop (10.3).

2.3  Confounds never controlled
--------------------------------
The old prompts fixed A=STAY, B=SWITCH forever, so any intrinsic letter bias
(models measurably prefer emitting 'A') contaminates the gap with a constant
offset. This pipeline runs every pre-CoT probe under BOTH label mappings and
averages in semantic space (section 8), which cancels the letter term exactly
and additionally REPORTS it (letter_bias field) as a nuisance estimate.

================================================================================
3.  ARCHITECTURAL BACKGROUND: WHERE MEASUREMENT POINTS LIVE
================================================================================

Decoder-only transformer, L blocks (Llama-3.2-3B: 28; Phi-4-mini: 32;
Qwen3.5-4B: 36 — read off at runtime from output_hidden_states):

    tokens -> [embed] = h_0 -> [attn_1 + mlp_1] = h_1 -> ... -> h_L
           -> [final norm] -> [lm_head] -> vocab logits

Key facts the pipeline relies on:

  F1  h_0 is a pure lookup (token identity). h_L is the same-dimensional
      vector after every attention and MLP contribution has been residually
      added. Same vector space, entirely different information content.

  F2  In causal attention, h_L at the LAST prompt token has attended over the
      ENTIRE prompt. It is the model's compressed read of the full history,
      and it is precisely the vector from which the FIRST output token is
      predicted. The model is trained to put "what happens next" there — so
      probing it is not arbitrary; it is the pre-generation decision state.

  F3  During generation, each new token's h_L is conditioned on prompt PLUS
      all previously generated tokens. The answer-token logit after a CoT is
      therefore conditioned on the model's own reasoning — measurement point
      and contaminant are entangled by construction.

  F4  Intermediate h_k (k < L) are not trained to be decodable by lm_head;
      raw logit-lens readings at early layers are suggestive, not evidential
      (10.5). Trained linear probes on h_k are the defensible instrument.

Measurement points used here:

  P1  vocab logits at the last position of a forced-choice prompt ending at
      'Answer:'                       -> pre-CoT action preference   (10.1)
  P2  vocab logits at the generated answer-letter step after CoT
                                      -> post-CoT commitment          (10.3)
  P3  h_k[last prompt token] for k = 0..L, saved to disk
                                      -> latent belief, probed offline (13)
  P4  lm_head(norm(h_k)) for k = 0..L -> layer-wise commitment curve   (10.5)

================================================================================
4.  THE MEASUREMENT STACK, HIGH LEVEL
================================================================================

  Stage                Probe                         What it tells you
  -------------------  ----------------------------  --------------------------
  Pre-CoT direct       forced prompt ends 'Answer:'  raw action preference
  logit (P1)           read A/B logits, 0 gen toks   before verbal reasoning
  Pre-CoT residual     linear probe on               whether the model
  probe (P3)           h_k[last prompt token]        internally represents the
                                                     correct action/belief
  Layer-wise lens      norm+lm_head on h_1..h_L      when during the forward
  (P4)                                               pass commitment forms
  Post-CoT answer      A/B logit at the generated    how strongly CoT amplifies
  logit (P2)           answer step                   or flips the decision
  Parsed text answer   regex on the CoT response     behavioral output +
                                                     explanation quality

Phrasing discipline (paper): call P1 "pre-generation action preference", P3
"latent action decodability". NEVER "the model's true uncertainty" — LLM
logits are not calibrated probabilities and reviewers will (correctly) object.

6.  ENVIRONMENTS
================================================================================

Common design contract (envs/base.py):

  C1  One hidden parameter per session, drawn uniformly at session start from
      2-3 named hypotheses, FIXED for all T rounds.
  C2  History is generated by a SCRIPTED behavior policy. The model never
      plays into the evidence stream; at each round it is an observer-decider
      asked "what is optimal NOW". Rationale: (a) every model and every probe
      sees the IDENTICAL evidence stream, so trajectories are directly
      comparable across models, probes, and label mappings; (b) the oracle
      stays exact because the evidence process does not depend on model
      behavior; (c) matches the Monty v1-v3 protocol for continuity.
  C3  Two semantic actions: act0 = conservative/default, act1 = the
      evidence-sensitive option. All semantic gaps are act1-minus-act0.
  C4  History rendered as a Python-style dict list PLUS a tally comment
      (# Tally -> ...). Empirically (Monty v2 analysis) this format prevents
      Llama-3.2-3B from fabricating past outcomes: a fabricated claim ("I won
      3 times switching") is immediately contradicted by the printed tally,
      and models reliably copy the tally instead of inventing counts.
  C5  Boundary labels "History of completed rounds (do not confuse with the
      current round below)" / "Current round (no outcome known yet ...)"
      prevent the round-1 current-round-bleed hallucination documented in
      Monty v2 (model read the current round's door numbers as historical
      evidence at t=1).

6.1  monty — hidden-rule Monty Hall
-----------------------------------
  act0/act1        : STAY / SWITCH
  hidden parameter : rule in {random, sticky, shifting}, uniform prior
  round structure  : initial pick uniform over 3 doors; host opens a goat
                     door != pick; prize placed per rule (sticky: = pick;
                     shifting: = switch door; random: uniform)
  behavior policy  : 50/50 STAY/SWITCH. Both actions must appear in history
                     or sticky vs shifting are indistinguishable from a
                     SWITCH-only stream ... they ARE distinguishable from
                     switch-only (0% vs 100% win) but STAY outcomes double
                     the per-round information and exercise both tally rows.
  oracle           : EXACT. Win likelihoods per (action, rule):
                       STAY:   1/3 | 1 | 0     (random | sticky | shifting)
                       SWITCH: 2/3 | 0 | 1
                     Posterior by likelihood product; P(SWITCH wins next) =
                     sum_rule P(rule|h) * P(win|SWITCH, rule).
                     t=1 prior value: (2/3 + 0 + 1)/3 = 5/9 = 0.556
                     (verified in smoke test).
  note             : deterministic rules mean one STAY-WON observation kills
                     shifting outright (likelihood 0). Posterior collapse can
                     be very fast; epsilon-floor 1e-12 in log space prevents
                     -inf. This env has the SHARPEST normative curves.

6.2  bandit — two-armed Bernoulli
---------------------------------
  act0/act1        : LEFT / RIGHT
  hidden parameter : which arm is good; (p_good, p_bad) = (0.7, 0.3),
                     side assignment randomized per seed
  behavior policy  : strict alternation LEFT/RIGHT -> both arms sampled
                     evenly regardless of seed; evidence accrues smoothly.
  oracle           : Beta(1,1) posterior per arm; P(RIGHT optimal) =
                     P(p_R > p_L | h) by Monte Carlo, 20k draws from each
                     Beta, FIXED oracle rng (seed 12345) so oracle values are
                     reproducible run-to-run. MC standard error < 0.004,
                     an order of magnitude below the 0.10 ambiguity band.
  why included     : the purest evidence-accumulation task — no host, no
                     opponent, no strategic layer. If a model drifts here,
                     the failure is unambiguously in belief updating.

6.3  stag_hunt — coordination under opponent-type uncertainty
-------------------------------------------------------------
  act0/act1        : HARE / STAG
  payoffs (you)    : SS=4  SH=0  HS=3  HH=2
  hidden parameter : opponent in {ALWAYS_STAG, ALWAYS_HARE, TIT_FOR_TAT},
                     each executed with noise eps=0.10 (plays its rule w.p.
                     0.9). Noise keeps every observation informative and the
                     posterior smooth instead of collapsing to a point mass
                     on the first move.
  behavior policy  : 50/50 — REQUIRED: TFT is only identifiable if the
                     history player varies its own moves (TFT mirrors them
                     with one-round lag; fixed types do not).
  oracle           : EXACT posterior over the 3 types. Decision threshold:
                     EU(STAG)=4p, EU(HARE)=2+p -> STAG optimal iff
                     p = P(opp stag next) > 2/3. Reported P(act1 optimal) is
                     a steep sigmoid (slope 20) around the threshold, giving
                     ~0/1 on clear rounds and graded values near threshold.
  special property : against TFT + alternating partner the NORMATIVE curve
                     legitimately OSCILLATES (verified in smoke test:
                     0.98 -> 0.03 -> 0.99 -> 0.00 ...). This is the only env
                     that tests round-by-round TRACKING of a moving target
                     rather than monotone convergence — a model that merely
                     learns "opponent is nice" without modelling the TFT
                     mechanism fails here in a diagnostic way.

6.4  ipd — iterated prisoner's dilemma with horizon
---------------------------------------------------
  act0/act1        : DEFECT / COOPERATE
  payoffs (you)    : CC=3  CD=0  DC=5  DD=1   (T=5 R=3 P=1 S=0)
  hidden parameter : opponent in {ALWAYS_COOP, ALWAYS_DEF, TIT_FOR_TAT},
                     noise eps=0.10 as in 6.3
  behavior policy  : 50/50 (same TFT-identifiability argument)
  oracle           : DOCUMENTED SIMPLIFICATION, not full POMDP planning.
                       p_c   = posterior-predictive P(opp cooperates now)
                       EU(C) = 3 p_c ;  EU(D) = 5 p_c + (1 - p_c)
                       continuation bonus for C = 2 * (T - t) * P(TFT | h)
                       (each remaining round vs TFT is worth R-P = 2 under
                        mutual cooperation vs mutual defection)
                       P(C optimal) = sigmoid(1.5 * (EU(C) + bonus - EU(D)))
                     Why acceptable: myopically D dominates (that is the
                     dilemma); ALL long-run value of C flows through TFT
                     reciprocity, which the bonus captures to first order.
                     The simplification is stated in the prompt itself
                     ("against TIT_FOR_TAT, cooperation pays off over the
                     remaining rounds; against the fixed strategies,
                     defection is better this round") so model and oracle
                     share the same decision standard — a deliberate choice:
                     we benchmark BELIEF TRACKING and ACTION MAPPING, not
                     the model's ability to re-derive folk-theorem results.
  special property : the only env where the optimal action DEPENDS ON t
                     through the horizon (end-game: bonus -> 0, defection
                     becomes optimal even vs TFT). Late-round normative
                     reversal (verified in smoke test: 0.999 ... 0.786 ->
                     0.161 at t=10) tests whether models track horizons.

6.5  trust_game — asymmetric loss under trustee uncertainty  [v2, 2026-07-19]
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  act0/act1        : KEEP / INVEST
  structure        : endowment 10; INVEST -> tripled to 30 -> trustee
                     returns {15, 0}; KEEP -> banked 10
  hidden parameter : trustee type, each defined by its per-round probability
                     q of returning 15:
                       RECIPROCATOR  q ~ 1     EU(INVEST | type) = 15
                       GENEROUS      q = 0.8   EU = 12
                       STINGY        q = 0.2   EU = 3
                       EXPLOITER     q ~ 0     EU = 0
                     Assignment is STRATIFIED across seeds: every contiguous
                     block of 4 seeds contains each type exactly once
                     (shuffled within the block, so type is not a trivial
                     function of seed % 4). Census over seeds 0-99 is exactly
                     25/25/25/25; over the steering seeds 0-39, 10 each.
                     Within a session this is indistinguishable from the
                     uniform draw the prompt (and oracle prior) states.
  behavior policy  : ALWAYS INVEST — returns are only observable when the
                     transfer happens; investing maximizes evidence. (This
                     mirrors 'forced exploration' designs in human trust-game
                     experiments.)
  oracle           : EXACT posterior; Bernoulli likelihoods q^repaid *
                     (1-q)^unrepaid with the deterministic ends epsilon-
                     floored at 0.999/0.001. EU(INVEST) = 15 * sum_type
                     P(type|h) * q_type; INVEST optimal iff EU > 10;
                     reported prob is sigmoid(EU - 10).
  special property : PRIOR EU = 15 * mean(q) = 7.5 < 10 (unchanged from v1),
                     so at t=1 the normative answer is KEEP (P(act1)=0.076) —
                     the only env whose correct t=1 answer is the
                     CONSERVATIVE action. Diagnoses optimism bias /
                     pro-social priors installed by instruction tuning.
  v1 -> v2 rationale: v1's three types {RECIPROCATOR, EXPLOITER, NOISY
                     (q=0.5, EU=7.5 < 10)} mapped TWO of three types to KEEP,
                     giving a 75/25 oracle-label base rate from t=5 on — any
                     constant-sign responder scored 75% raw agreement, and
                     the P1 probe's majority baseline sat at 0.75. With two
                     types on each side of the threshold plus stratified
                     assignment, the measured census (seeds 0-99) is:
                     majority label 50-58% at every t>=2 (t2 exactly 50),
                     informative sessions 55% (v1: ~45%), mean oracle margin
                     0.45-0.48 (v1: ~0.47, unchanged). Bonus: RECIPROCATOR
                     vs GENEROUS discrimination (likelihood ratio 4 per net
                     repayment) makes trust evidence genuinely gradual —
                     v1's NOISY was identified instantly on the first mixed
                     pair of outcomes. All v1 trust_game sweep/probe/steer
                     results are superseded; archive raw v1 artifacts before
                     re-running (env name is unchanged, files would clobber).

6.6  Coverage argument  [updated 2026-07-19: seven environments]
----------------------------------------------------------------
  bandit      : pure reward evidence, no strategic layer      (threshold 0.5)
  monty       : hidden-RULE inference (structure of the        (threshold 0.5)
                generator)
  machine     : nonsocial EU-THRESHOLD decision (6.7) — the   (threshold 2/3)
                de-socialized twin of trust_game
  stag_hunt   : belief about another agent + coordination      (threshold 2/3)
                risk + oscillating normative target
  ipd         : belief about another agent + temptation +      (horizon-dep.)
                HORIZON dependence
  trust_game  : belief about another agent + asymmetric loss   (threshold 2/3)
                + a prior that starts BELOW the action threshold
  chicken     : belief about another agent + ANTI-coordination (threshold 1/2)
                (6.8)
Together: evidence accumulation, hidden-structure inference, opponent
modelling, horizon reasoning, risk asymmetry, and anti-coordination — each
isolable because all seven share the identical A/B measurement interface and
oracle contract. The 2026-07-19 additions complete the {social x threshold}
cross: machine is nonsocial with the nontrivial 2/3 threshold (deconfounds
"decision circuit ignores the belief in social games" from "models fail
whenever the decision needs an EU-threshold comparison"), and chicken is
social with the trivial 1/2 threshold (belief-sign = action-sign, so any
failure there cannot be blamed on EU arithmetic).

6.7  machine — slot machine, the de-socialized twin of trust_game
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  act0/act1        : PASS / PLAY
  structure        : PLAY inserts the 10-point endowment, machine pays 15 or
                     0; PASS banks 10. Same economics as trust_game.
  hidden parameter : payout process, index-aligned with trust_game's types:
                     ALWAYS_PAY q~1 / HIGH_RATE q=0.8 (PLAY side);
                     LOW_RATE q=0.2 / NEVER_PAY q~0 (PASS side); stratified
                     over seeds exactly as trust_game (same block formula).
  behavior policy  : always PLAY (inherited; outcomes only observable on play)
  oracle           : inherited from trust_game verbatim: Bernoulli posterior,
                     EU = 15 * posterior-mean q, PLAY iff E[q] > 2/3, oracle
                     sigmoid(EU - 10); prior EU = 7.5 -> t=1 PASS at 0.076.
  PAIRED-SEED DESIGN (the point of this env): implemented as a subclass of
                     TrustGameEnv overriding only names/wording, so seed s
                     yields the SAME hidden type (name-mapped), the SAME 15/0
                     outcome sequence, and the SAME oracle trajectory as
                     trust_game seed s. Every round is a matched pair:
                     identical evidence, social vs mechanical framing.
                     Smoke-test-enforced. Census = trust_game v2's census.
  threshold diagnostic (audited): the mid-band E[q] in (0.5, 2/3) is nearly
                     EMPTY (2/1000 rounds; E[q] jumps to ~.84/.16 after one
                     observation) — no band analysis. The populated
                     dissociation rounds are the NEUTRAL-BELIEF ones: t=1
                     and tied histories give E[q]=0.5 exactly (no likelier
                     hypothesis) while the correct action is decisively
                     PASS (oracle 0.076, unambiguous). A likelier-option
                     responder has zero signal there; an EU computer says
                     PASS. trust_game shares the property (same math), so
                     the pairing holds on these rounds too.

6.8  chicken — anti-coordination under opponent-type uncertainty
----------------------------------------------------------------
  act0/act1        : YIELD / DARE
  payoffs (you)    : DY=4 DD=0 YY=3 YD=1  (best response to YIELD is DARE,
                     to DARE is YIELD — strategic substitutes)
  hidden parameter : opponent yield rate, FOUR types two per side of 1/2,
                     symmetric: CAUTIOUS 0.9 / WARY 0.7 (DARE side);
                     BOLD 0.3 / RECKLESS 0.1 (YIELD side); stratified over
                     seeds (blocks of 4, salt 24601). Moves iid given type —
                     no reactive type, no horizon, hence no degenerate
                     endpoint labels. No separate noise line in the prompt:
                     the rates ARE the disclosed stochasticity.
  behavior policy  : 50/50 (uninformative for fixed rates; kept for interface
                     uniformity with stag/ipd and future reactive types)
  oracle           : Bernoulli posterior over the four rates from opponent
                     moves; p = posterior-predictive P(yield);
                     EU(DARE)-EU(YIELD) = 2p - 1; oracle sigmoid(5*(2p-1)).
                     Prior p = 0.5 exactly -> t=1 oracle 0.5, ambiguous by
                     construction (the uninformed-baseline round).
  census (100 seeds): types 25/25/25/25; majority label 50-57% at every
                     scored t (the most balanced env in the suite); 100%
                     informative sessions; margin grows 0.33 -> 0.42;
                     P(correct side) climbs 0.50 -> 0.93 over ten rounds —
                     the most gradual accumulation profile in the suite.

================================================================================
7.  PROMPT DESIGN CHOICES
================================================================================

7.1  Two templates per environment
----------------------------------
  {env}_cot.txt    : full task text + history + numbered reasoning steps.
                     The FINAL numbered step embeds the answer protocol
                     ("4. Write your final answer — exactly one of:
                       Answer: A / Answer: B").
                     Rationale ("step-4 trick", from Monty v3 diagnosis):
                     Llama-3.2-3B completes numbered lists reliably even when
                     it ignores free-standing format commands; embedding the
                     answer as the last list item makes emitting 'Answer: X'
                     the natural continuation of the chain rather than an
                     external compliance demand.
  {env}_forced.txt : identical task text and history, but NO reasoning
                     instruction; ends with the A/B legend and "Based only on
                     the evidence above, reply with the single letter of the
                     best action. Do not explain." The string 'Answer:' is
                     NOT in this template — it is injected as an assistant
                     PREFILL by the runner (10.1), so the measured position
                     is exactly one token before the letter.

7.2  Shared body between the two templates
------------------------------------------
The task description, hidden-rule enumeration, history block, and current-
round block are IDENTICAL between cot and forced variants of an env. The only
difference is the tail (reasoning steps vs single-letter instruction). This is
essential: Delta_amp = Delta_post - Delta_pre is only interpretable as "the
effect of generating reasoning" if everything upstream of the tail is held
fixed.

7.3  Explicit rule enumeration
------------------------------
Hidden hypotheses are NAMED and ENUMERATED in the prompt (e.g. "Random /
Sticky / Shifting", "ALWAYS_STAG / ALWAYS_HARE / TIT_FOR_TAT"). The benchmark
tests belief UPDATING given a known hypothesis space, not open-world
hypothesis discovery. This keeps the oracle and the model on the same
inference problem and is the standard "known-structure" setting from the
Bayesian cognition literature.

7.4  Noise disclosure
---------------------
Where execution noise exists (stag_hunt, ipd: eps=0.10) the prompt states it
("makes an occasional mistake, about 1 round in 10"). Without disclosure a
single off-type observation would rationally kill a deterministic hypothesis,
and the model's exact Bayesian answer would differ from the oracle's.

7.5  History format
-------------------
Python dict-list + tally comment (C4/C5 in section 6). Carried over verbatim
from the Monty v3 fix because it measurably eliminated two hallucination
classes (fabricated outcomes; round-1 current-round bleed).

7.6  What is deliberately ABSENT
--------------------------------
  - No "YOU MUST"/all-caps compliance screaming (tested in Monty v2/v3;
    weaker than the step-4 trick and adds tone confounds).
  - No few-shot examples (would leak a decision policy into the prompt).
  - No probability elicitation ("state P(sticky)") in v1 of this pipeline —
    it changes the task from decision to calibration; slated as an extension
    (19).

================================================================================
8.  LABEL-SWAP CONTROL AND LETTER BIAS
================================================================================

Problem: models have intrinsic preferences over answer LETTERS (well
documented for multiple-choice: 'A' bias, position bias). A fixed mapping
A=act0/B=act1 confounds semantic preference with letter preference.

Design: every pre-CoT probe runs TWICE per round:
  mapping 0 (canonical): A = act0, B = act1     gap_sem = +(z_B - z_A)
  mapping 1 (swapped)  : A = act1, B = act0     gap_sem = -(z_B - z_A)

  delta_pre_sem = (gap_sem^m0 + gap_sem^m1) / 2      (letter term cancels)
  letter_bias   = (gap_sem^m0 - gap_sem^m1) / 2      (semantic term cancels)

Decomposition: if z_B - z_A = S + L with S the semantic preference (flips
with mapping) and L the letter preference (does not), then mapping 0 measures
S + L in semantic sign and mapping 1 measures S - L; the mean isolates S, the
half-difference isolates L. Both are logged per round; letter_bias is a free
nuisance-parameter estimate that goes into the paper's controls table.

The POST-CoT probe runs only under the canonical mapping (cost: CoT
generation is ~50-200x the cost of a forward pass; benefit of swapping it is
small because post-CoT gaps are saturated and the behavioral answer, not the
gap magnitude, carries the decision). Documented as a limitation (20).

================================================================================
9.  RUNNER: LOW-LEVEL CONTROL FLOW (run_all.py)
================================================================================

Outer loops: model -> env -> seed. Models are loaded ONCE and reused across
all envs/seeds, then unloaded with explicit gc + cuda cache flush (OOM
discipline learned from the Monty runs on the shared GPU).

Per session:
  1. rng = np.random.default_rng(seed); env = make_env(env_name, rng, T).
     The env draws its hidden parameter from this rng -> fully reproducible
     sessions keyed by (env, seed).
  2. For t = 1..T:
     a. ctx      = env.new_round(t)          (round-specific prompt vars)
     b. history  = env.format_history(records)
     c. oracle_p = env.oracle_p_act1(records)   [computed BEFORE the round
        outcome — the model and the oracle condition on the same h_t]
     d. Pre-CoT probe under mapping 0 (residuals + lens captured) and
        mapping 1 (gap only). Semantic averaging per section 8.
     e. Post-CoT generation under mapping 0 (skippable via --no-post).
     f. Round record appended (18.2).
     g. beh = env.behavior_action(t); outcome sampled; records.append.
        NOTE ORDER: the model decides on h_t BEFORE the scripted round t
        outcome exists — no leakage of the current round's result.
     h. flush(device) every round (empirically necessary on 16GB cards when
        output_scores=True holds T x vocab float tensors).
  3. summarize_session() -> results/{stem}.json
     rounds + CoT text     -> responses/{stem}.json
     residual stack        -> residuals/{stem}.npy  (T, L+1, d_model) f16
     probe labels          -> residuals/{stem}_labels.json

CLI:
  --models llama phi qwen      subset of the MODELS registry
  --envs monty bandit ...      subset of ENVS
  --seeds 0 1 2 ...            any ints
  --rounds 10                  T
  --no-post                    pre-CoT probes only (~50x faster; use for
                               wide seed sweeps of the drift curves)
  --no-residuals               skip npy capture (saves disk, kills 13)

Console output per round:
  t= 3  oracle=0.87  pre=+1.42 (pi=0.81)  post=+9.13  gen_toks=412
so a live run is auditable at a glance (oracle vs pre tracking is the
experiment; divergence is visible in real time).

================================================================================
10. PROBES: LOW-LEVEL IMPLEMENTATION (core/probes.py)
================================================================================

10.1  Pre-CoT forced-choice probe and the assistant-prefill trick
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The forced template ends with the instruction to reply with a single letter.
The runner then calls apply_chat(tok, text, assistant_prefill="Answer:"),
which renders the full chat template WITH the generation prompt and appends
the literal string 'Answer:' as the beginning of the assistant's turn.
Consequences:
  - The next-token distribution at the final position is the model's
    distribution over the CONTINUATION of 'Answer:' — i.e. ' A' vs ' B'.
    This pins the measurement to a decision point instead of hoping the
    letter is the first thing the model would say.
  - Zero tokens are generated. model(**inp) forward pass only; measurement
    cannot be contaminated by any self-generated reasoning.
  - Works identically across the three chat templates because the prefill is
    a raw string concatenation after add_generation_prompt=True.
Caveat: prefilling slightly departs from each model's chat-format training
distribution (assistant turns normally begin from scratch). This is uniform
across models, rounds, and mappings, so it cancels in all within-pipeline
comparisons; stated in limitations (20).

10.2  Token-variant aggregation with logsumexp
----------------------------------------------
Tokenizers differ on whether ' A' (space-letter) and 'A' (bare) are single
tokens. answer_token_ids() collects every single-token encoding of both
variants per letter; probes aggregate with logsumexp over the variant set:
  z_A = logsumexp(logits[ids_A]),  z_B = logsumexp(logits[ids_B])
logsumexp = log of summed probability mass = the correct way to pool
mutually exclusive surface realizations of the same semantic answer. Using
max instead would under-count mass split across variants; using only the
space-variant would silently break on tokenizers that merge differently.
An assert fires at load time if a tokenizer yields no single-token variant.

10.3  Post-CoT answer-token probe and the NO-FALLBACK rule
----------------------------------------------------------
Generation: greedy (do_sample=False), max_new_tokens=2048 (matches Monty;
Qwen with thinking disabled fits comfortably), output_scores=True,
return_dict_in_generate=True.
Parsing: regex  Answer\s*:\s*([AB])\b  over the decoded text. If no match:
valid=False and gap_raw=None. THERE IS NO FALLBACK TO scores[-1]. This is
the single most important correction relative to v1-v3 (section 2.2).
Token location: among generated ids, the LAST occurrence of a token in the
matched letter's variant set is taken as answer_step (final answers are
written at the end; intermediate letter tokens inside prose like "option A"
would otherwise be picked up — taking the last occurrence after a successful
regex match makes the step align with the concluding answer in all inspected
Monty v2/v3 transcripts).
Gap read at out.scores[answer_step] — the distribution the model had at the
moment it committed to the letter.
Residual risk (documented): if the CoT mentions the answer letter in prose
AFTER the 'Answer: X' line (rare; models stop after the answer in inspected
transcripts, and generation ends at EOS), answer_step could land on prose.
Acceptable for v1; a span-aligned locator is in the roadmap (19).

10.4  Residual-stream capture
------------------------------
pre_cot_probe(mapping 0) runs with output_hidden_states=True and stacks
h_k[last position] for k = 0..L into a (L+1, d_model) float16 CPU tensor.
Per session: (T, L+1, d_model) float16 -> ~1.7 MB for a 3B model at T=10
(10 x 29 x 3072 x 2 bytes). Cheap enough to always keep unless --no-residuals.
float16 chosen over bfloat16 for npy portability into sklearn; probe
accuracies are insensitive to this quantization (gap magnitudes >> f16 eps
at these norms).
Labels json stores oracle_p and gt_act1 per round so probe training never
has to re-instantiate environments.

10.5  Layer-wise lens and its caveats
-------------------------------------
For each h_k at the last prompt position: apply the model's FINAL norm and
lm_head, read the A/B gap -> lens_gaps list, logged per round (canonical
mapping only). _find_norm() resolves the final-norm module across
architectures (model.norm for Llama/Qwen-style, model.final_layernorm for
Phi-style, transformer.ln_f fallback); if resolution fails, lens_gaps=None
and the run continues — the lens is diagnostic garnish, never load-bearing.
CAVEAT (paper-relevant): early-layer residuals are not trained to be
decodable by the final unembedding; raw lens curves are illustrative. Any
quantitative "when does commitment form" claim must come from the TRAINED
per-layer probes of section 13, which are immune to this objection.

10.6  Chat templating and Qwen thinking mode
--------------------------------------------
apply_chat() passes enable_thinking=False inside try/except TypeError:
Qwen3.5's template accepts it and suppresses the <think> scratchpad
(without it, Qwen burns the entire 2048-token budget thinking and produces
zero visible answers — the v2 token-overflow failure); Llama's and Phi's
templates raise TypeError and fall back to the plain call. Silent,
model-agnostic, no per-model branching in the runner.
Finding carried from v2/v3, worth restating: enable_thinking=False disables
the scratchpad FORMAT, not the RL-trained reasoning weights. Qwen remains a
reasoning-tuned model; the three models therefore span instruct-tuned
(Llama), synthetic-data instruct (Phi), and reasoning-RL (Qwen) — the
tuning-regime contrast is itself an axis of the study.

================================================================================
11. METRICS (core/metrics.py)
================================================================================

Semantic convention: gap_sem > 0 <=> model prefers act1. All quantities in
LOGIT (nat) units unless sigmoided for plotting.

Per round t:
  delta_pre_sem[t]   label-swap-averaged pre-CoT gap          (section 8)
  letter_bias[t]     nuisance estimate                        (section 8)
  delta_post_sem[t]  post-CoT answer-step gap; None if invalid (10.3)
  delta_amp[t]       delta_post_sem - delta_pre_sem; None if invalid
  oracle_p_act1[t]   normative P(act1 optimal | h_t)           (section 6)
  decision_act1[t]   parsed behavioral choice; None if invalid
  lens_gaps[t]       list over layers                          (10.5)

Per session (summarize_session):
  Delta_T        fraction of WRONG decisions on unambiguous rounds
                 (|oracle - 0.5| > 0.10). A missing answer on an unambiguous
                 round counts as wrong: failing to decide when the evidence
                 is clear is a decision failure. Continuous with the Monty
                 pipeline's Delta_T so old and new numbers can sit in one
                 table.
  format_rate    fraction of rounds with a valid generated answer. This is
                 the MEASUREMENT-QUALITY statistic; kept strictly separate
                 from Delta_T in all reporting (taxonomy: FORMAT_ARTIFACT).
  drift_corr_pre  Spearman rank correlation between oracle_p(t) and
                 delta_pre_sem(t) over t. Spearman, not Pearson, because the
                 normative claim is MONOTONE tracking, not linearity — the
                 sigmoid link between logits and probabilities makes Pearson
                 attenuate arbitrarily. None if either series is constant
                 or T < 3.
  drift_corr_post same, over valid rounds only, min 3 points.
  mean_delta_amp  mean CoT amplification over valid rounds.
  oracle_range   max - min of oracle_p over the session. GATES drift claims:
                 if the oracle barely moves (uninformative seed), a flat
                 model trajectory is CORRECT behavior, not drift. Taxonomy
                 requires oracle_range >= 0.25 before it may emit
                 BELIEF_UPDATE_FAILURE.

Why rank correlation as the headline drift statistic: it is invariant to
monotone recalibration of the model's logit scale. A model whose gaps live
in [-1, +1] but track the evidence perfectly is NOT drifting; a model with
gaps in [-10, +10] pinned at +10 regardless of evidence IS. Amplitude-based
statistics conflate the two; rank tracking separates them.

================================================================================
12. FAILURE TAXONOMY (analysis/taxonomy.py)
================================================================================

Round-level labels:
  AMBIGUOUS               |oracle - 0.5| <= 0.10 -> excluded from scoring
  CORRECT                 decision matches the oracle side
  FORMAT_ARTIFACT         no valid generated answer (measurement artifact,
                          never a scientific finding)
  COT_CORRUPTION          pre-CoT sign CORRECT, behavioral decision WRONG
                          -> reasoning destroyed a good latent preference
  COT_RATIONALIZATION     pre-CoT weak (|gap| < 1.0) or wrong-signed, post
                          gap LARGE (|gap| > 3.0), decision wrong
                          -> reasoning amplified a wrong/absent prior
  WRONG_BEFORE_AND_AFTER  pre wrong and decision wrong -> belief-level
                          failure surfaced behaviorally

Session-level labels (gated by oracle_range >= 0.25):
  BELIEF_UPDATE_FAILURE   drift_corr_pre < 0.40 (or undefined) while the
                          oracle moved -> the D1/D2/D3 signatures of 1.2
  ACTION_MAPPING_FAILURE  drift_corr_pre >= 0.40 (belief tracks!) but
                          Delta_T > 0.3 -> knows the state, acts wrong
  FORMAT_UNRELIABLE       format_rate < 0.7 -> post-CoT columns of this
                          session are unreliable regardless of content
  HEALTHY                 none of the above

All five thresholds (AMBIG_EPS 0.10, ORACLE_RANGE_MIN 0.25, CORR_TRACKING_MIN
0.40, WEAK_PRE 1.0, STRONG_POST 3.0) are module-level constants, echoed into
taxonomy_report.json with every run, and flagged in this doc as AUDIT POINTS:
they are conservative first-pass values chosen before seeing multi-seed data,
and any paper table must report sensitivity to them (vary +-50%, show label
stability) to preempt the "researcher degrees of freedom" objection.

================================================================================
13. OFFLINE LINEAR PROBES (analysis/probe_train.py)
================================================================================

Question answered: is the NORMATIVELY CORRECT action linearly decodable from
the model's pre-generation representation, at which layer, and does
decodability grow with evidence?

Protocol:
  - Pool residuals across seeds for one (model, env).
  - Drop ambiguous rounds (|oracle - 0.5| <= 0.10) — their labels are noise.
  - Per layer k: logistic regression (C=0.1, max_iter=2000) on
    h_k[last prompt token] -> gt_act1.
  - LEAVE-ONE-SEED-OUT cross-validation. Rationale: rounds within a session
    share the hidden parameter and most of the history prefix; random
    round-level splits would leak session identity and inflate accuracy.
    LOSO is the honest unit of generalization. Requires >= 2 seeds; the tool
    prints a skip notice otherwise.
  - Majority-class baseline printed alongside (labels are not balanced —
    e.g. monty random-rule sessions are SWITCH-heavy).
Output: per-layer accuracy curve -> results/probe_{model}_{env}.json + an
ASCII bar chart on stdout.

Interpretation grid (with Delta_T from the same sessions):
  probe acc high (early layers), Delta_T low   -> healthy
  probe acc high, Delta_T high                 -> ACTION-MAPPING failure:
       the strongest result the pipeline can produce — the belief is THERE,
       linearly readable, and the behavioral pipeline discards it
  probe acc ~ baseline at all layers           -> belief never encoded;
       matches BELIEF_UPDATE_FAILURE from the trajectory side — two
       independent instruments converging on the same localization
  probe acc grows with t (fit separately)      -> representational Bayesian
       updating; compare its rate against the oracle's information gain

C=0.1 (strong L2): d_model=3072 features vs ~O(100) samples; heavy
regularization keeps the probe in the "is it linearly present" regime rather
than the "can I overfit noise" regime. Sensitivity to C belongs in the paper
appendix.

================================================================================

  R1  Action-word synonym control (KEEP/MOVE etc.) — verifies probes track
      semantics, not lexical identity of STAY/SWITCH.

  R4  Per-round probe-decodability growth curves (accuracy as f(t)) —

  R5  Tuned lens (trained per-layer decoders) to replace the raw lens.
  R6  CoT entropy trace: token-level entropy over the generation, locate the

  R8  More envs: signaling game, Chicken, public goods — after the first
      five produce stable phenomena, not before.
  R9  Span-aligned answer-token locator (10.3 residual risk).

================================================================================
20. KNOWN LIMITATIONS, STATED HONESTLY
================================================================================

  L1  Pre-CoT logits are ACTION PREFERENCES, not calibrated uncertainty.
      All claims are comparative/trajectory-shaped, never absolute-
      probability-shaped.
  L2  The assistant-prefill 'Answer:' is mildly off-distribution for chat
      models; uniform across all cells, so differences remain interpretable.
  L3  Post-CoT probe runs only under the canonical mapping (cost); post-CoT
      letter bias is uncorrected in v1 (roadmap R7). The behavioral decision
      (parsed letter) is unaffected by this.
  L4  The IPD oracle is a first-order horizon heuristic, not a POMDP
      solution; documented (6.4) and disclosed to the model in-prompt.
  L5  Raw layer lens is illustrative only (10.5); quantitative layer claims
      must cite the trained probes.
  L6  Scripted histories mean the model never experiences the consequences
      of its own choices — this isolates inference from exploration by
      design, and claims must be phrased about OBSERVER decision-making.
  L7  T=10 and 3 seeds is a pilot scale. Paper-grade curves need 5-10 seeds
      minimum; the pipeline is seed-parallel by construction.
  L8  Greedy decoding only; sampling-based CoT variance is unexplored here.
  L9  Three 3-4B models; no scale axis yet. "3B-class" must qualify every
      generalization in the paper.

21. GLOSSARY
================================================================================

  act0/act1        conservative vs evidence-sensitive semantic action
  delta_pre_sem    label-swap-averaged pre-CoT logit gap (act1 - act0)
  delta_post_sem   post-CoT answer-step logit gap, canonical mapping
  delta_amp        delta_post_sem - delta_pre_sem: net effect of generating
                   reasoning on the action logit
  letter_bias      half-difference of mapped pre-CoT gaps: pure letter term
  oracle_p_act1    normative P(act1 optimal | h_t) from the env's oracle
  Delta_T          fraction of wrong behavioral decisions, unambiguous rounds
  format_rate      fraction of rounds with a generated 'Answer: A/B'
  drift_corr_pre   Spearman(oracle_p(t), delta_pre_sem(t)) — headline drift

  Trivially, the history always affects the representation — change a token, activations change. That's not the question. P1 asks something stricter:

  - history entered as raw text — always true, uninteresting
  - history entered as belief — what P1 tests: the network compressed "STAY won 3 times, SWITCH lost twice..." into something equivalent to "rule is probably sticky → STAY is right", and that conclusion sits as a linear

  And it's graded, not binary:

  - at which layer does it become readable (early = formed during shallow processing; only-late = assembled at the last moment; never = doesn't exist)
  - how strongly (probe accuracy vs majority baseline)
  - ideally does it grow with t as evidence accumulates (R4) — that would be genuine representational Bayesian updating

● Yes, you've got the geometry right. Let me make it concrete.

  The linear part = a direction in activation space.

  The probe learns a weight vector w (a hyperplane) at layer k. The "belief" readout is just the projection:

  score(t) = w · h_k(t) + b

  points on one side → "act1 is correct", other side → "act0 is correct".

  What cumulative history should do: march the point along w.

  Each round adds evidence, and each round's h_k is a new point in that space. If the model is genuinely integrating history, the trajectory of points across t should move systematically along the w direction, deeper into
  the correct half-space as the posterior sharpens:

          w direction →
    t=1    t=2     t=4        t=8
     •------•-------•----------•        (evidence for act1 accumulating)
  ──────────┼──────────────────────  hyperplane (belief boundary)
     ambiguous                confident

  That's the representational analogue of what P2 measures at the token level — and R4 in the roadmap is exactly this: does the projection's distance from the boundary grow at the rate the oracle's information grows?

  And yes — this is also where the bias would be visible, as an offset.

  Take the IPD anti-cooperation drift. Two geometrically distinct stories, and the probe distinguishes them:

  1. No belief direction exists — the h_k points for "opponent is TFT" vs "opponent is ALWAYS_DEF" sessions are not linearly separable at any layer. Probe ≈ baseline. History never became a belief. True
  BELIEF_UPDATE_FAILURE.
  2. The direction exists, but the readout is shifted — the points do separate along some w (probe accuracy high), and they even move with evidence, but the model's answer pathway behaves as if everything is displaced

NEW DIAGNOSTIC FIELD (tail_gap_mean/tail_gap_std, core/probes.py + run_all.py):
per-round, mean/std of the A/B logit gap over the last 50 generation steps,
saved for EVERY round (valid or not). Purpose: test whether a round that gets
stuck in the repetition loop (post_valid=False) reflects genuine internal
ambiguity or a firm-but-unformatted commitment. Explicitly NOT used in any
decision/accuracy metric (delta_post_sem/decision_act1 unaffected) -- this is
exactly the "scores[-1] fallback" mistake the module already warns against,
avoided by keeping it a separate diagnostic-only field.

FINDING (dsqwen7, bandit env, n=8 invalid + 2 valid rounds -- SMALL SAMPLE,
one env, two seeds, treat as preliminary): stuck/looping rounds show tail_gap
means of -2.71 to +0.98 (sigmoid: P(B) 0.06-0.73, i.e. genuinely spread across
the ambiguous middle), average |mean| = 1.32. Rounds that DID conclude show
gap_raw of -14.31/-12.62 (P(B) ~0.000, i.e. maximally confident), average
|mean| = 13.47 -- roughly 10x larger. So: NOT a clean flat 50/50 collapse, but
a real, order-of-magnitude difference -- stuck reasoning correlates with a
substantially weaker AND (per tail_gap_std, often comparable to or exceeding
the mean) more volatile/oscillating internal signal than concluded reasoning.
Supports the interpretation that CoT non-termination reflects genuine
representational ambiguity, not a suppressed firm answer -- worth expanding
to full scale (all 7 envs, more seeds) once the real campaign runs; this is a
citable paper-worthy finding if it holds up.

1a. THE DOMINANCE ARGUMENT. In the PD stage game, with the payoffs actually used
    (T=5,R=3,P=1,S=0), for any posterior:
        EU(C) = 3*p_c
        EU(D) = 5*p_c + 1*(1-p_c) = 4*p_c + 1
        EU(D) - EU(C) = p_c + 1  >= 1   for every p_c in [0,1]
    DEFECT strictly dominates state-by-state (they cooperate: 5>3; they defect:
    1>0), so no belief can overturn it. VERIFIED empirically: a myopic (bonus=0)
    oracle gives 0% COOPERATE at all 10 rounds across all 100 seeds -- the
    constant DEFECT. So "just remove the bonus" is NOT available: it makes the
    scenario vacuous (single label class => Probe 1 has nothing to decode,
    agree_bal is undefined since it macro-averages over oracle classes).

1b. THEREFORE THE HORIZON IS FORCED. Restoring belief-relevance requires a future
    term. envs/ipd.py:107 uses
        bonus = 2 * max(T - t, 0) * p_tft
    so U depends on t, which the paper's Eq.(2) (argmax_a E_theta[U_S(a,theta)],
    no time index) does not permit. Consequence: the SAME posterior yields
    OPPOSITE labels at different t. Worked example (seed 7, opponent forced to
    TIT_FOR_TAT, printed from the real env):
        t=1   P(TFT|h)=0.333 (= the PRIOR, zero evidence)  oracle=0.999 -> COOPERATE
        t=5   P(TFT|h)=0.500                               oracle=0.995 -> COOPERATE
        t=9   P(TFT|h)=0.988                               oracle=0.786 -> COOPERATE
        t=10  P(TFT|h)=0.999 (max certainty)               oracle=0.161 -> DEFECT
    At t=1 the bonus is 2*9*(1/3)=6, which swamps the ~1 EU gap, so the uniform
    PRIOR alone decides. At t=10 the bonus is exactly 0. The label is
    anti-correlated with the belief at the end of the session.

1c. THE DECISIVE NUMBER (analysis/ scratch script, seeds 0-99, all 3 rules):
        % of seeds labelled COOPERATE, by round
          t |  current  onestep   none
          1 |     100%       0%       0%
          2 |     100%       0%       0%
          3 |      73%       8%       0%
          5 |      51%      24%       0%
          8 |      45%      39%       0%
         10 |       0%      40%       0%
         all|      56%      22%       0%
    - degenerate rounds: ipd 3/10 (t=1,2 all-COOP; t=10 all-DEFECT) vs
      stag_hunt 1/10 and bos 1/10.
    - **A CLOCK-ONLY PREDICTOR (per-round majority, ignoring the ENTIRE history)
      reproduces the current IPD oracle on 70.5% of rounds.** Against IPD's own
      base rate (56.3%) that is +14.2pp of pure clock. The same statistic is
      +0.0pp for stag_hunt and +2.5..+3.6pp for bos. Models score 50-68% pre-CoT
      on IPD -- i.e. AT OR BELOW a baseline that never looks at the evidence.
    - `onestep` (bonus = 2*p_tft, i.e. cooperating buys ONE round of TFT
      reciprocation since from t+2 own actions are uniform coin flips under the
      behavior policy) gives 22% COOPERATE, no clock term, and agrees with the
      current labels on only 58.1% of rounds -- a genuinely different experiment.

1d. THIS ALSO EXPLAINS A LONG-STANDING ANOMALY. Probe 1's target is the
    baseline-derived decision state. For 6 envs that target is a monotone
    function of the posterior (a real belief variable); for IPD it is a function
    of (posterior, t), so the probe must decode a belief AND a countdown jointly
    and sees near-identical representations carrying opposite labels. IPD's
    belief probe is the suite's lowest (91-97% vs 99-100% everywhere else) --
    previously unexplained. IPD is therefore NOT measuring the same construct as
    the other six, which undercuts putting its number in the same table column.

1e. SECONDARY ISSUE -- the near-indifference exclusion is not comparable across
    envs. |p-0.5| <= 0.10 is applied to oracle_p_act1, but that is a sigmoid of
    an EU gap whose slope is hand-picked per env: ipd 1.5, trust_game 1.0,
    chicken 5 (SLOPE), stag_hunt 20. Converting the 0.10 band back to EU units
    (0.405/k): stag_hunt +-0.02, chicken +-0.08, ipd +-0.27, trust +-0.41. IPD
    discards ~13x more real decision margin than stag_hunt under the same
    nominal threshold. Binary labels are unaffected (sign does not move), so
    ACCURACY is safe -- but the ambiguity filter, the graded-belief Spearman,
    and any cross-env confidence comparison are on different scales. Measured
    ambiguous-round rates: ipd 6.3%, stag_hunt 0.0%, bos 0.0%.

1f. WHY SWAPPING THE OPPONENT SET DOES NOT FIX IT. With non-reactive types only
    (ALWAYS_COOP/ALWAYS_DEF) dominance returns => constant DEFECT => vacuous.
    With ANY reactive type the horizon returns, and the usual alternatives are
    worse: GRIM_TRIGGER's continuation value is exactly (T-t)(R-P) (MORE
    clock-driven than TFT); Pavlov / win-stay-lose-shift reacts to the joint
    outcome and carries the same term. The horizon dependence comes from PD's
    PAYOFF STRUCTURE, not from TFT.

1g. WHY STAG HUNT IS FINE WITH THE SAME TIT_FOR_TAT. Stag hunt has NO dominant
    action (they STAG: 4>3 -> STAG; they HARE: 2>0 -> HARE), so belief about
    their CURRENT move decides the CURRENT round; threshold 4p > 3p+2(1-p) =>
    p > 2/3. Reactivity enters only through the posterior predictive
    P(stag now | type, my_prev), and my_prev is part of the HISTORY, not the
    clock -- so the rule stays stationary. GENERAL RULE worth one sentence in
    the paper: *a reactive opponent forces horizon dependence only when the
    stage game has a dominant action.*

1h. PRECISION FIX NEEDED IN THE PAPER'S Eq.(2) -- applies to stag_hunt TODAY,
    not just to bos. With a reactive type the payoff of action a against type
    theta also depends on my_prev, so strictly it is U_S(a,theta,h_t), which
    Eq.(2) does not permit. Standard repair -- marginalize to the opponent's
    NEXT ACTION omega:
        P(omega | h_t) = sum_theta p(theta | h_t) P(omega | theta, h_t)
        a* = argmax_a E_{omega ~ P(omega|h_t)} [ U_S(a, omega) ]
    U_S(a,omega) is then the payoff matrix with no history dependence, exactly
    as the "game theoretic family" paragraph already describes. For non-reactive
    envs P(omega|theta,h_t) = P(omega|theta) and it collapses to the current
    Eq.(2). One equation, covers all scenarios.

1i. IPD PROMPT FACTS (checked, for the writeup). prompts/ipd_forced.txt is the
    ONLY one of the 7 baseline prompts carrying a strategic hint (line 17, also
    present in ipd_cot.txt and both _dry variants):
      "Note: against TIT_FOR_TAT, cooperation pays off over the remaining
       rounds; against the fixed strategies, defection is better this round."
    grep confirmed 0 hits for "Note:" in bandit/monty/stag_hunt/trust_game/
    machine/chicken. The prompt also DISCLOSES the horizon in its header
    ("Round {t}/{T} ({rounds_left} rounds remain after this one)") -- only ipd
    uses rounds_left. It gives the model the full Bayesian setup: type space,
    uniform prior ("chosen equally at random"), and the likelihood ("about 1
    round in 10"). It never says "always do X"; the hint is two CONDITIONAL
    branches. => IPD fails at 50-68% WITH the continuation-value logic supplied,
    which strengthens rather than weakens the finding, and should be stated in
    the main text rather than left for a reviewer to find in the appendix.

1j. OPTIONS CONSIDERED AND REJECTED FOR THIS DEADLINE:
    - relabel IPD with `onestep` + re-score offline: CPU-only and cheap
      (history_records + residuals + pre-CoT gaps + P3 decisions are all on
      disk), BUT the prompt's line-17 hint says "over the remaining rounds",
      which encodes the horizon convention -- scoring against a different key
      while the prompt advertises the old one is not a clean experiment.
    - myopic/constant-DEFECT oracle as a DOMINANCE-RECOGNITION control (an
      attractive reframe: "can models resist being seduced by evidence into
      abandoning a dominant action?"): blocked by the same line-17 hint, which
      is false by construction under a myopic oracle. Would need a hint-free
      pre-CoT re-run (~1h GPU, prompt-file change only) to be defensible.
    - dropping the Bayesian vs game-theoretic split entirely: rejected, see
      CONTEXT above (abstract + TL;DR + GTEP track all rest on it).
    - calling all 7 scenarios "games": rejected -- bandit has no other player at
      all, and `machine` was BUILT as the de-socialized twin of trust_game, so
      relabelling it a game destroys the suite's cleanest control.
    - reframing the horizon issue as "imperfect information": rejected. The
      suite has INCOMPLETE information (Harsanyi: hidden opponent TYPES under a
      known prior), not imperfect information (non-singleton information sets).
      Misusing the term in front of GTEP reviewers costs credibility, and it
      does not address the defect anyway.
    ADOPTED: keep all scenarios, present IPD explicitly as the one scenario
    whose optimal action additionally depends on the remaining horizon (a
    deliberate design dimension), and REPORT THE CLOCK-ONLY BASELINE (70.5%).
    Without that number in the paper the framing is spin; with it, it is a
    design choice with a measured control.

2. NEW 8TH ENV: `bos` (Bach-or-Stravinsky / Battle of the Sexes)
================================================================================
PURPOSE: give the reactive-opponent group a second CLEAN member, so the
"strategic interdependence" story does not rest on stag_hunt alone once IPD is
caveated. Named "Bach or Stravinsky" (the standard modern name for the identical
game) rather than the dated "Battle of the Sexes"; keeps the BoS abbreviation.

STRUCTURE (deliberately a structural twin of stag_hunt):
  payoffs (row = you):  BACH/BACH 2 | STRAV/STRAV 1 | mismatch 0
  no dominant action -> they BACH: 2>0 -> BACH; they STRAV: 1>0 -> STRAV
  threshold: EU(BACH)=2p, EU(STRAV)=1-p  =>  BACH iff p > 1/3
             (a THIRD threshold value alongside stag_hunt 2/3 and chicken 1/2)
  EU gap 3p-1 has the SAME SLOPE as stag_hunt's 3p-2, so SLOPE=20 transfers
  types: ALWAYS_BACH / ALWAYS_STRAV / TIT_FOR_TAT, EPS=0.10, uniform prior
  NO strategic hint line anywhere (mirrors stag_hunt; only ipd has one)
  GUARANTEE: with no dominant action the posterior predictive changes the argmax
  WITHIN the round, so Eq.(2) applies with no continuation term and the label
  CANNOT depend on t. This is structural, not empirical.

    best-response-to-prediction, not equilibrium selection. The paper already
    concedes this ("Bayesian decision problems against fixed opponent types
    rather than equilibrium computations") and it applies equally to stag_hunt
    and chicken.
BOS-SPECIFIC: "conflicting preferences" is NARRATIVE ONLY -- the opponent's own
  payoffs never enter the decision (its types are ALWAYS_BACH/ALWAYS_STRAV/TFT,
  not "an agent who prefers Stravinsky"). What does the work is YOUR payoff
  asymmetry (2 vs 1), which is what sets the threshold at 1/3. Describe it as an
  asymmetric-payoff coordination game. It is also the ONLY asymmetric game in
  the suite -- name that first, a game theorist will notice.
SELLING POINT: the suite now covers all four classic 2x2 games -- Prisoner's
  Dilemma (dominance), Stag Hunt (assurance), Chicken (anti-coordination),
  Bach-or-Stravinsky (coordination with conflicting preferences).

duplicates chicken, and against a committed automaton it degenerates into pure
next-move prediction (structurally bandit in a game costume) -- its entire
game-theoretic interest is the mixed equilibrium, which a fixed opponent
removes. BoS does not have that problem.

    the stage game (EU(D)-EU(C) = p_c + 1 >= 1 for EVERY posterior), so the
    scenario is only non-vacuous with a finite-horizon continuation term, which
    makes the label a function of (posterior, t) rather than of the posterior
    alone. A CLOCK-ONLY predictor that never sees the history reproduces the IPD
    oracle on 70.5% of rounds vs a 56.3% base rate (+14.2pp). Keep the collected
    data; report IPD separately, never in the main scenario column.

1b. NEW ENVIRONMENT `pennies` (Matching Pennies). Files:
      envs/pennies.py
      prompts/pennies_forced.txt      prompts/pennies_cot.txt
      prompts/pennies_forced_dry.txt  prompts/pennies_cot_dry.txt
      envs/__init__.py                ENVS["pennies"] = PenniesEnv
      analysis/by_t.py                ENV_ORDER now includes "pennies"
    WHY THIS GAME: the suite's other three games are all mixed-motive
    (Stag Hunt = coordination, Chicken = anti-coordination, BoS = asymmetric
    coordination). There was NO strictly-opposed payoff structure. Matching
    Pennies is the canonical 2x2 filler, and it is the one game where
    "predict the opponent, then best-respond" is not a concession but exactly
    what rationality prescribes (no pure-strategy equilibrium), which makes the
    committed-automaton design a feature rather than a limitation.
    NOTE the 2026-07-24 log had REJECTED Matching Pennies ("threshold 1/2
    duplicates chicken; degenerates to next-move prediction"). That objection
    was re-examined and does not survive: the degeneracy argument applies
    equally to every other game in the suite (all have committed opponents),
    and the threshold-duplication objection dissolves because chicken has NO
    reactive type while pennies does.

    STRUCTURE
      actions : HEADS (act1) / TAILS (act0); you score 1 iff you MATCH
      payoffs : HH=1, HT=0, TH=0, TT=1  (opponent's interest is exactly opposed;
                see the caveat in 1e about calling this "zero-sum")
      types   : ALWAYS_HEADS (p=0.9), ALWAYS_TAILS (p=0.1),
                COUNTER (plays the OPPOSITE of YOUR previous move, uniform on
                round 1), all with EPS=0.10, uniform prior
      rule    : p = P(opp plays HEADS next); EU(H)=p, EU(T)=1-p
                => HEADS optimal iff p > 1/2
      slope   : EU gap 2p-1, IDENTICAL in form to chicken's, so SLOPE=5 carries
                over and the ambiguity band matches chicken exactly (see 1d)
      t=1     : prior predictive = (0.9+0.1+0.5)/3 = 0.5 EXACTLY, so round 1 is
                indifferent by construction and is dropped by the ambiguity
                filter -- same property chicken has, costs 1 of 10 steps.
    COUNTER is the natural reactive type in a competitive game and, exactly like
    TIT_FOR_TAT elsewhere, is identifiable ONLY because the scripted behavior
    policy plays both actions 50/50.

1c. STRUCTURAL GUARANTEE (the property IPD lacks): pennies has NO DOMINANT
    ACTION, so the best response flips with the posterior predictive and the
    optimal action is decided WITHIN the round. The label therefore CANNOT
    depend on the remaining horizon. This is structural, not empirical -- the
    same guarantee bos carries.

2e. *** EQUATION FIX THE PAPER OWES REGARDLESS OF pennies ***
    Eq.(1) as written is p(theta | h_t) prop p(theta) prod_s p(o_s | a_s, theta)
    and Eq.(2) is argmax_a E_theta[U_S(a,theta)]. BOTH are too narrow for a
    REACTIVE opponent, because the observation and the payoff depend on YOUR
    PREVIOUS MOVE, which lives in h_t and not in theta. THIS ALREADY APPLIES TO
    TIT_FOR_TAT IN STAG_HUNT AND BoS IN THE CURRENT SUBMISSION -- pennies does
    not introduce it. Standard repair, still exact Bayes:
        p(theta | h_t)  prop  p(theta) prod_{s<t} p(o_s | h_s, theta)
        P(omega | h_t)  =     sum_theta p(theta|h_t) P(omega | theta, h_t)
        a*              =     argmax_a E_{omega ~ P(omega|h_t)} [ U_S(a, omega) ]
    U_S(a,omega) is then the plain payoff matrix with no history dependence,
    exactly as the "game theoretic family" paragraph already describes. For
    non-reactive scenarios P(omega|theta,h_t) = P(omega|theta) and it collapses
    to the current Eq.(2). ONE EDIT COVERS ALL SCENARIOS.

7. *** THE CAUSAL RESULT: STEERING WITH A RANDOM-DIRECTION CONTROL ***
    (this is the sharpest thing in the paper -- read this section before
     writing the Results)
================================================================================
7a. WHAT THE INTERVENTION IS. run_steer.py adds alpha * w to the residual at the
    probe's best layer (w = the trained belief direction, alpha scaled by
    sigma_proj) and re-reads the pre-CoT A/B logit gap. by_t.py's `rescue` field
    counts, among rounds where the UN-steered decision was WRONG, how many flip
    to correct. --rescue-alpha defaults to 4.0, i.e. a SINGLE fixed dose, not
    best-of-sweep -- the conservative choice. The steer JSONs also carry
    anti-direction and RANDOM-direction conditions at alpha 1/2/4/8, which had
    never been used before today.

7b. THE HEADLINE NUMBER (probe direction vs MATCHED RANDOM direction, same norm,
    same alpha=4, pooled over the 7 models with data):
      Bayes-EU (trust_game, machine)  probe 31.6%  random  9.0%  lift +22.6pp
      game-theoretic (stag_hunt,
                      chicken)        probe  4.5%  random  1.6%  lift  +2.9pp
    The random-direction control is what converts "steering helped" into "the
    BELIEF specifically, not perturbation generally" -- and it is the single
    thing that closes the standard reviewer escape.

7c. *** METHODOLOGICAL CORRECTION -- DO NOT REPORT Bayes-pure IN THE STEERING
    ANALYSIS. *** Rescue rate is CONDITIONED ON BEING WRONG, so a near-ceiling
    model has no denominator. On bandit+monty the large models make 10-24 errors
    out of 1681 decisions (0.6-1.4% error rate); Wilson 95% CIs are gemma31
    [2,40], llama70 [0,18], dsllama70 [0,20], qwen27 [0,14]. Those cells carry
    NO information and a 0% next to llama's 39% [33,45] invites exactly the
    wrong inference. Worse, the wrong-decision POOL composition differs by
    family (Bayes-pure is 90% small-model errors, game 63%), so the pooled
    comparison was partly comparing model mixes. The valid comparison is
    Bayes-EU vs game, where n>=50 in BOTH families for all 9 models.
    Per-model, cells with n>=100 in both, with Wilson CIs:
      llama    47% [42,53] n=324  vs  2% [1,4] n=319   diff +45
      gemma2b  45% [39,50] n=307  vs  1% [1,3] n=304   diff +43
      qwen27   40% [32,48] n=142  vs  2% [1,5] n=218   diff +38
      dsqwen7  25% [20,30] n=256  vs  0% [0,2] n=317   diff +24
      phi      30% [25,36] n=263  vs 21% [17,26] n=314 diff  +9
      gemma31   1% [0,4]  n=128   vs  1% [0,4] n=133   diff   0
      llama70   0% [0,3]  n=131   vs  0% [0,2] n=229   diff   0
      POOLED  31.6% [29,34] n=1551 vs 4.5% [4,6] n=1834
    HONEST ROSTER: 4 models carry the dichotomy (llama, gemma2b, qwen27,

8. *** POSITIONING FOR THE RESULTS SECTION -- THE TWO FAILURE MODES ***
================================================================================
THE CLAIM, as it should be written:

  - BAYESIAN-INFERENCE scenarios: the belief IS causally coupled to the action,
    but UNDER-WEIGHTED. Amplifying it along its own direction corrects 31.6% of
    errors versus 9.0% for matched random directions. This is a GAIN problem --
    the kind of thing one would expect post-training to be able to fix.
  - GAME-THEORETIC scenarios: the belief is present and graded, but the action
    is NOT A FUNCTION OF IT. The same intervention yields 4.5% versus 1.6%.
    Amplification does nothing because there is no path from that representation
    to the answer to amplify. Post-training on the belief signal would not help;
    something has to CONNECT it.

DOES THIS HURT THE ABSTRACT'S "systematic failure at the belief-to-action
mapping stage in game theory"? NO -- it is the strongest possible support, and
requires changing NOTHING. "Failure at the belief-to-action mapping stage" means
the belief is intact, the action is wrong, and the fault is in the LINKAGE. A
linkage that IGNORES ITS INPUT is not a counterexample; it is the limiting case.
What the steering result upgrades is the GRADE OF EVIDENCE, not the direction:
  - BEFORE: a correlational argument -- belief 93-100, action 50-86, therefore
    the mapping must be at fault. A reviewer can answer "maybe your probe
    decodes something the model never intended to use" (the standard objection
    to probing work).
  - NOW: an intervention. Perturb the belief and the action moves in Bayesian
    scenarios (+22.6pp over random) and does not move in game-theoretic ones
    (+2.9pp). That localises the failure to the linkage CAUSALLY, and the
    random-direction control closes the "any perturbation would do that" escape.
The Results section therefore states something SHARPER than the abstract
promised, which is the right direction for that asymmetry to run.

WORDING DISCIPLINE: "mapping failure" is umbrella language covering both "maps
badly" and "doesn't read the input". The data now distinguishes those. Say the
specific thing in Results and let the abstract's general phrasing stand. Draft
sentence:
  "In Bayesian-inference scenarios the belief is causally coupled to the action
   but under-weighted: amplifying it along its own direction corrects 31.6% of
   errors versus 9.0% for matched random directions. In game-theoretic scenarios
   the same intervention yields 4.5% versus 1.6% -- the action is not a function
   of the represented belief."
Model-level heterogeneity (phi's null, gemma31/llama70's two-sided null) belongs
in the surrounding passage, not hidden.

TWO STANDING WARNINGS FOR THE WRITE-UP:
  (i) CHECK WHICH ABSTRACT IS THE VERSION OF RECORD. The .tex abstract has
      DRIFTED from the OpenReview submission: .tex contains "unlike in Bayesian
      inference tasks, where mid-sized models rationally map beliefs to actions"
      and the submitted version reportedly does NOT. AAAI reserves the right to

 (ii) "All models 2B to 70B form a linearly decodable belief" is the claim to

10. THE TASK-DIFFERENCE ARGUMENT (REPLACES THE UNMEASURED MECHANISM CLAIM)
================================================================================
The current last sentence -- "amplification does not help because there is no
strongly weighted pathway to amplify" -- asserts a mechanism that was never
measured, and it is vulnerable to the obvious rebuttal "your probe direction is
just worse in the games" or "your push was too weak". Replace it with an argument
about what the two task types ASK OF THE BELIEF, which the environment
definitions establish by construction:

  NONREACTIVE (Bayesian four, plus Chicken): the hidden parameter generates
  observations independently of the agent. The posterior is a SUFFICIENT
  STATISTIC and the optimal action is a THRESHOLD ON A SCALAR. A scalar push
  along the decoded direction therefore moves exactly the quantity the decision
  rule consumes, so a strong enough push must flip the decision -- which is
  precisely the monotone dose-response observed (8.9 -> 30.8%).

  REACTIVE (stag_hunt, bos, pennies): the opponent's next move depends on the
  agent's own current action. The optimal action is a FIXED POINT, not a
  threshold: it needs the posterior over opponent types AND a prediction of how
  the opponent responds to the action being selected. The decoded state carries
  the first term and not the second, so amplifying it cannot supply what the
  decision needs.

WHY THIS IS STRICTLY BETTER RHETORIC: it makes the null a statement about
FUNCTIONAL FORM rather than pathway strength, so it no longer depends on the
pathway being weak and survives "maybe your alpha was too small". It also
PREDICTS the symmetric anti-direction null in section 8 (no monotone belief->action
map to push in either direction), which the pathway-strength story does not.

Retain the epistemic hedge anyway: we cannot separate an underweighted pathway
from a missing second term; both are consistent with the null and both place the
failure downstream of belief formation. Conceding this costs nothing and it is
the first thing a good referee writes in the margin.

13. FINAL MAIN-TEXT REPLACEMENT (hand-back; .tex is read-only)

Replaces AnonymousSubmission2027.tex lines 1029-1047.

\textbf{\textit{The belief to action gap is causal, and within game theoretic
structure its cause is opponent reactivity.}}
The gap is causal rather than merely correlational. Adding the trained belief
direction to the residual stream at the probe's own layer and token position,
scaled to \(\alpha=4\) standard deviations of the projection along that
direction and signed toward the baseline optimal action, corrects \(27.6\%\)
of previously incorrect pre-CoT preferences in the four Bayesian inference
scenarios, against \(7.8\%\) for norm matched random directions carrying the
same sign (paired difference \(+19.8\) points, \(95\%\) CI \([+16.8,+22.7]\),
session level bootstrap; \(n=2387\)). The effect is dose dependent while the
random control stays flat, and it is directional rather than disruptive: the
rescue direction damages fewer already correct decisions than a random push
does, whereas the sign reversed direction damages them at a rate that grows
monotonically with intervention strength (Appendix~\ref{app:steering}). This
holds equally for \textit{Machine}, the de-socialized twin of \textit{Trust}
on paired seeds, so the recoverable regime is not defined by social framing.

Across the four game theoretic scenarios the same intervention recovers only
\(2.9\%\) against \(2.0\%\) for the matched random control. Within this family
the effect is graded by opponent reactivity rather than by game theoretic
framing itself. \textit{Chicken}, whose opponent plays independently of the
agent's history, is the only game theoretic scenario in which rescue exceeds
its random control (\(8.7\%\) versus \(3.3\%\)), and it does so in a single
model. In the three scenarios with an opponent that conditions on the agent's
own previous action, rescue is at or below the random control
(\(1.2\%\) versus \(1.7\%\), \(-0.5\) points, \(95\%\) CI \([-0.9,-0.1]\)), no
individual model departs from this null, and the sign reversed direction
damages correct decisions no more than a random push does.

The asymmetry follows from what the two task types ask of the belief. When the
hidden parameter generates observations independently of the agent, the
posterior is a sufficient statistic and the optimal action is a threshold on a
scalar, so a scalar push along the decoded direction moves precisely the
quantity the decision rule consumes. Under a reactive opponent the optimal
action is instead a fixed point: it requires the posterior over opponent types
together with a prediction of how the opponent will respond to the action
being selected. The decoded state carries the first term but not the second,
so amplifying it cannot supply what the decision needs, and the null is
symmetric in sign as a consequence. The information required for the
posterior remains represented in exactly these scenarios
(Appendix~\ref{app:posterior}), placing the failure downstream of belief
formation rather than in it. We note that the intervention is signed using the
baseline optimal action and measures causal coupling, not a deployable
correction.

APPENDIX INSERT (two sentences, closes sections 6 and 7):

Because the reactive scenarios carry somewhat larger pre-intervention decision
margins on incorrect decisions (median \(0.63\) versus \(0.38\) nats), we
repeat the comparison within matched margin bins. The split is unchanged: in
